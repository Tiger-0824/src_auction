Samal Rout Cup ®
Rules & Regulations
[September - 2026] Sunday 27TH September, 2026
Dear Esteemed Participants,
We would like to remind all esteemed participating teams and players of the following comprehensive and rigorous rules and guidelines for the upcoming cricket tournament:
Part - 1 The official ICC T20I rules and match guidelines will be strictly and meticulously followed throughout the entirety of the tournament.
Part - 1 The decisions made by the match umpires are final, absolute, and binding. No arguments or disputes against an umpire's call will be tolerated.
Part - 1 Major sledging, taunting, or the use of abusive language is strictly prohibited. Proper decorum and high standards of sportsmanship are expected from all contestants.
Part - 1 The use of offensive or vulgar language is completely banned. The tournament aims to maintain a dignified, respectful, and family-friendly environment for all players and spectators.
Part - 1 To establish an equitable and simplified playing field, there will be no leg byes or LBW (Leg Before Wicket) dismissals applicable in this tournament.
Part - 1 Boundaries on the sides will count for standard 4 and 6 runs.
Part - 2 However, no automatic 4 runs will be awarded for shots hit directly behind the wicketkeeper and the point area.
Part - 3 In these specific zones, batsmen must physically run between the wickets to score.
Part - 1 A maximum time limit, as officially communicated by the organizers, is allocated to complete a single innings of play.
Part - 2 Strict adherence to this limit ensures optimal time management, tournament scheduling, and equal playing opportunities for all competing sides.
Part - 1 Every team is required to bring their own playing kit and gear to ensure fair competition. Voluntarily sharing kits demonstrates commendable sportsmanship and will be rewarded with Fair Play points.
Part - 2 Teams do not need to bring match balls, as they will be supplied for all fixtures. However, practice balls will not be provided by the organizers.
Part - 3 If a ball is lost during active match play, a suitable replacement ball will be provided by the tournament organizers.
Part - 1 Pace bowling in any form is strictly banned. Full pace-chucking is prohibited; only spin-chucking is permissible. Round-arm bowling actions are not allowed under any circumstances.
Part - 2 Batsmen are permitted to make a formal, genuine appeal to the match umpire regarding a bowler's delivery pace or illegal bowling action.
Part - 3 All bowling actions must uphold the highest ethical standards, fair play, and integrity of the game.
Part - 1 Each team must consist of the exact squad size specified and mandated by the tournament organizers.
Part - 2 A total of 2 or 3 teams will participate in the tournament.
Part - 3 Final team rosters, grouping, and player assignments will be determined exclusively through an official auction process.
Part - 1 Both the main umpire and the leg umpire will continuously monitor the bowling actions of all players throughout the match.
Part - 2 This dual-umpire oversight ensures strict compliance with tournament guidelines, guaranteeing absolute fairness and delivery legality at all times.
Part - 1 The last remaining batsman is permitted to continue batting alone once all other wickets have fallen.
Part - 2 This provision ensures consistent team participation and allows the batting side to maximize its innings.
Part - 1 No registration refunds will be issued if a match is called off, interrupted, or abandoned due to unforeseen circumstances or adverse conditions.
Part - 2 This policy is strictly enforced to maintain organizational accountability and cover pre-allocated event logistics.
Part - 1 Incoming batsmen must be ready to take strike at the crease within one (1) minute of the previous batsman being dismissed.
Part - 2 Strict enforcement of this timer ensures the smooth, efficient, and continuous progression of the match without unnecessary delays.
Part - 1 Any player caught engaging in cheating, unfair practices, or severe behavioral misconduct will be instantly banned from the tournament by the organizers.
Part - 2 Banned or absent players cannot be replaced or substituted by a new player under any circumstances.
Part - 1 If a bowler delivers two (2) waist-high full-toss no-balls (beamers) during a match, they will be immediately suspended from bowling for the remainder of that game.
Part - 2 This enforcement strictly adheres to official ICC regulations regarding dangerous and unfair bowling.
Part - 1 Each team will bowl a designated total of specified overs per match as determined and announced by tournament organizers.
Part - 2 Under no circumstances is any individual bowler permitted to exceed their strict per-match over limit.
Part - 1 If any player is found engaging in a physical or verbal fight with another player, they will be instantly banned from participating for the remainder of that active innings.
Part - 2 This immediate sanction is strictly enforced to uphold match safety, de-escalate conflict, and maintain order on the field.
Part - 1 If either the striker or the non-striker leaves their respective crease before the ball is declared dead, the fielding team retains the full right to dismiss the player via a run-out.
Part - 2 Batters must maintain strict crease discipline and ensure the ball is officially deemed dead by the umpire before stepping outside the crease.
Part - 1 Running out the non-striker while backing up too far before the ball is bowled (commonly known as "Mankading") is fully legal, permitted, and recognized as a legitimate dismissal.
Part - 2 This procedure strictly adheres to the updated ICC playing regulations. No prior warning to the non-striking batter is required before the fielding side executes the dismissal.
Part - 1 Only the exact number of active fielders designated by the organizers will take the field during a bowling innings (comprising 1 bowler, 1 wicketkeeper, and the specified outfielders).
Part - 2 Additional squad members must remain on the team bench and may only be utilized as substitutes for fatigued or injured fielders in accordance with tournament specifications.
Part - 1 To ensure strict time management, a penalty of one (1) run per minute will be applied against any team responsible for delaying the scheduled start of a match.
Part - 2 These penalty runs will be credited directly to the opposing team to discourage preventable delays and maintain the tournament timetable.
Part - 1 The official scheduled start time for all tournament matches is set for 06:15 AM.
Part - 2 A strict grace period is provided up until 07:00 AM to allow for final pitch inspection and team preparation.
Part - 3 If match commencement is delayed even one minute past 07:00 AM, run penalties will immediately begin accumulating as outlined in Clause 22.
Mandatory Attire Note: Full team jersey, proper trousers, and standard sports shoes are strictly mandatory to take the field.
